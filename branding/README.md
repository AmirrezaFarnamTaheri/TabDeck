# TabDeck brand assets

The TabDeck identity uses an original stacked-tab deck symbol.

- `TabDeck-mark.svg` — square standalone mark for app, release, and connector use.
- `TabDeck-lockup.svg` — horizontal product lockup.

Palette:

- deep navy `#071B2A`
- teal `#0F766E`
- bright teal `#14B8A6`
- warm amber `#F59E0B`
- off-white `#F8FAFC`

Keep the mark undistorted, preserve its internal spacing, and maintain clear space equal to at least one front-card corner radius. The artwork is part of the TabDeck source distribution and may be used with unmodified TabDeck builds.
